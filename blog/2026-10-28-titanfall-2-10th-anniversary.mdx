Unfortunately we don't have a release date for Titanfall: Assault to announce today but rest assured it will be soon™!

If you haven't been paying attention to the #tfa-progress channel here's where we're at now:

{/* truncate */}

- Everything outside of battles is implemented and working, though will need further testing to ensure there's no bugs.
- Depending on the map and units picked battles are nearly fully implemented! It's possible to play a full match with some issues.

There's still some major inaccuracies with the battles that need to be fixed, notably with movement and attacking.
Due to the game being designed around a deterministic lockstep model there's zero room for error and getting this right might take a while.
But we'll get there!
